About the Trophy

The UEFA Champions League trophy is one of the most prestigious awards in world football. It is awarded annually by the Union of European Football Associations (UEFA) to the winner of the Champions League competition.

UEFA Champions League Trophy

The trophy, nicknamed "Big Ears" due to its distinctive handles, is 74 cm tall and weighs 7.5 kg. It is crafted from silver and awarded to the winning team.
